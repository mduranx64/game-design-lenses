# Example: three-player salvage

**Intended interaction:** One player scouts a route, one stabilizes machinery, and one transports cargo. Roles rotate at a safe station so a newcomer can learn without being permanently assigned a minor job.

**Incentive risk:** Paying only the carrier encourages everyone to claim that role. A shared completion reward plus visible individual contributions is a candidate fix; test whether players still feel useful.

**Dropout case:** If the stabilizer leaves, a temporary slower mechanism lets the team retreat or finish. The remaining players can reassign roles.

**Griefing case:** A player repeatedly drops cargo into deep water. Inspect the action's consequences and consider recoverable drops in shared safe areas or limits on destroying shared cargo. Preserve intentional tactical throwing where the game needs it.
