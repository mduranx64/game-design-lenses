# Example: a compact lighthouse approach

**Topology:** Dock -> Courtyard -> Pump Room -> Tower. Courtyard -> Rooftop -> Tower is an optional route. A shortcut connects Tower back to Dock after activation.

**Dependencies:** Pump control in the Courtyard drains the Pump Room. No item in that room is needed to activate its own drain.

**Meaningful routes:** Pump Room offers shelter and a resource cost; Rooftop offers a faster but exposed traversal. Both must be checked with the player's actual movement abilities.

**Guidance:** The rotating light reveals the Tower from the Dock. A broken pipe visually connects the flooded room to its control. Returning players recognize the opened shortcut through a changed gate silhouette.

**Test:** Observe the first wrong turn and whether players recover using environmental information. Separately verify roof jumps in a blockout; adjacency alone cannot establish their reachability.
