# Example: a harbor at dusk

**Experience:** Cautious exploration interrupted by moments of welcome.

**Visual rule:** Navigable platforms have a stable, simple silhouette. Decorative wreckage may be intricate but stays lower in contrast. Friendly doorways use a recurring warm shape; danger cannot rely on a red/green distinction alone.

**Audio role:** A distant bell provides orientation. Footstep changes acknowledge safe landing. Music recedes around the bell so its direction remains readable. A visible landmark supports players who cannot use the sound.

**Production test:** Build or sketch one view containing the destination, one threat, one interaction, and decorative clutter at the expected gameplay size. Ask fresh viewers to identify each. A mood board alone would not answer that readability question.
