# Example: first-use gate confusion

**Supplied observations:** In build A, 3 of 5 fresh players attempted the locked gate before seeing its control. Two found the control unaided; one needed a hint. All five eventually passed.

**Supported finding:** Gate/control discoverability caused difficulty for at least one participant and may have affected three. Eventual completion alone hides the assistance.

**Unknown:** Whether those first attempts were normal exploration or frustrating confusion. The notes do not establish emotion or long-term engagement.

**Next test:** Use the same neutral starting instruction with fresh players. Compare a visible connection between gate and control against the current presentation. Record first actions, discovery time, assistance, and the player's explanation of the connection.

**Avoid:** 'Players hate the gate' or 'the revised gate is proven intuitive' without the corresponding evidence.
