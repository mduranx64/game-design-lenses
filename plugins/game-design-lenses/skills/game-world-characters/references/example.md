# Example: the flood archivist

**World rule:** Written ink fades in floodwater; etched marks survive. This changes how clues and records can be stored.

**Character function:** The archivist teaches this rule and asks the player to choose which records to preserve.

**Trait in action:** Meticulous but easily overwhelmed. They maintain precise shelves yet freeze when several rooms flood at once. A player action helps them prioritize rather than simply declaring them 'more confident'.

**Directed relationship:** The boatmaker regards the archivist as impractical; the archivist depends on the boatmaker but resents damaged records. A shared rescue can change trust without making every disagreement disappear.

**Playable evidence of change:** After the player saves a set of records, the archivist supplies a durable etched route card and delegates the next rescue. That behavior communicates a shift the player can notice.
