# Example: a prototype funding request

**Pitch core:** A cooperative rescue game where players read rising water and decide whom to help together. A short prototype will test whether divided information creates useful communication.

**Ask:** Resources for a clearly scoped prototype milestone; team, budget, and schedule remain user-supplied inputs.

**Illustrative arithmetic only:** Fixed cost 120,000; net receipts 12 per unit after stated deductions; additional variable cost 2 per unit. Contribution is 10, so break-even is 12,000 units. At net receipts 10 it becomes 15,000 units. Neither figure predicts sales.

**Client feedback:** 'Add more characters' may mean the experience feels too repetitive. Confirm the concern and compare additional characters with a cheaper variation in meaningful decisions.
