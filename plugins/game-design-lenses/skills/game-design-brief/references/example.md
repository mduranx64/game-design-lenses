# Example: lantern delivery

Original example created for this plugin; the details below are design hypotheses.

**Request:** A small game about delivering lanterns through a flooded village, with a feeling of thoughtful generosity.

**Experience promise:** Read a neighbor's situation, choose a route through rising water, and see a dark home become welcoming because of the delivery.

**Loop:** Observe requests and water -> choose recipient and route -> spend travel time -> deliver or retreat -> see changed paths and household needs.

**Theme alignment:** The mechanics reward considerate planning; households react to deliveries; warm light contrasts with dark water; a paper map and tokens can test the route decisions.

**First experiment:** Give players three requests, two routes, and a visible tide timer. Watch whether they weigh who needs help against safe passage. If they always take the closest house without reading requests, revise the consequences before adding more houses.

**Boundary:** No farming, crafting tree, or open-world expansion is needed to test this promise.
